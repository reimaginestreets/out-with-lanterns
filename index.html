Dear Momma,

Emily Dickinson wrote the line quoted at the top of this page in a letter to a friend while settling into a new house. She used it to capture the sensation of not being able to find anything in the chaos of unpacking. It has since taken on a life of its own, with people on the internet suggesting a deeper meaning of finding oneself. Both interpretations seemed right for you in this moment.

I hope you’ll use this site as a place to find your way back to yourself gently, graciously, and, most of all, lovingly, through reflection, movement, and stillness. Nothing here is counted or timed. I can’t see what you type; anything you write stays on the device you’re using. You might see changes every once in a while as I come up with new ideas (and you of course are welcome to suggest changes!), but the foundation will stay the same (and whatever entries you make won’t be erased).

While it’s meant to be cute, I hope it’s also something worth savoring: slowly, with intention, and when you need a pick-me-up. You are on an epic journey, personally and professionally, and if this online journal can help as you navigate the winding road you find yourself on, it will have fulfilled its purpose.

With all my love, forever and always, to the moon and beyond,

Tonashay
